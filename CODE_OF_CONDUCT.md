# Code of Conduct

GovernLoop welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, levels of experience, tools, and viewpoints.

## Our standard

Project discussions should be:

- respectful and focused on the work
- specific about evidence, assumptions, and tradeoffs
- open to correction and independent review
- safe for people to disagree without personal attacks
- mindful that security reports and private credentials require confidential handling

Constructive behavior includes asking clarifying technical questions, providing reproducible evidence, acknowledging uncertainty, and separating critique of a change from judgment of a contributor.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly exposing secrets/security vulnerabilities in a way that creates avoidable harm.

## Governance discussions

GovernLoop intentionally distinguishes technical evidence from authorization. Disagreement about governance decisions is welcome when expressed with concrete reasoning and without attempting to bypass repository controls or pressure maintainers into unsafe actions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and communications that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue containing personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies in GovernLoop repository spaces and project-related interactions where a participant is representing the project.
